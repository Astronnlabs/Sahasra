Questions

How do I buy the plan?

Sign up, then choose monthly, quarterly or yearly on the plan page inside the software. You’ll pay on PayU’s secure page and come straight back to SAHASRA with your plan active.

I already have an account. How do I renew?

Log in and open Settings → Subscription & Billing → Renew plan. The new time is added on top of the days you have left.

What happens when my plan ends?

Your data stays safe. You’ll see the plan page when you log in, and everything opens again as soon as you renew.

Which payment methods can I use?

UPI, debit and credit cards, and net banking, through PayU.

How do WhatsApp e-bills work?

Connect your VAAHAK account in SAHASRA (Settings → E-Billing). Each bill is then sent to your customer on WhatsApp from your own business number, and charged to your VAAHAK wallet.
