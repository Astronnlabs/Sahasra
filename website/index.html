What you get with SAHASRA

Everything a shop owner needs every day, without spreadsheets or a paper register.

Fast POS billing

GST invoices in seconds with barcode search, discounts, UPI QR, cash, card, credit and split payments. Print on any thermal or A4 printer.

Live inventory

Stock goes down with every sale and up with every purchase. Low-stock alerts, batches and expiry, serial numbers and stock adjustments.

GST ready

Sales and purchase registers, HSN summary, credit and debit notes, and tax reports ready for your accountant.

Customers and vendors

Customer history, credit sales and dues. Purchases, vendor bills and payments in one place.

Staff and payroll

Attendance, advances and monthly salaries. Give each staff member a login with only the screens they need.

Reports

Today’s sales, profit, expenses, dues and top items on one dashboard. Export to Excel any time.

WhatsApp e-bills

Send every bill to your customer on WhatsApp automatically by connecting your VAAHAK account. Less paper, more repeat customers.

Bill Studio

Design your own bill with your logo, address, terms and UPI QR, for thermal or A4 printers.

Safe and backed up

Your data is stored securely in the cloud with role-based access and a full audit log of who changed what.
